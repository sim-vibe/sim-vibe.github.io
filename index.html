What is Keyz?

A mechanical keyboard
in your pocket.

Keyz is a keycap fidget app — a pocket-sized set of mechanical keyboard keycaps you can tap anytime for the satisfying click, clack, and thock of real switches. No typing, no rules: just tap the keys, feel the haptics, hear the switch sounds, and make the set your own with custom labels, images, and skins. It's a fidget toy, a keyboard ASMR machine, and a tiny customizable playground — free on iOS and Android.

What is a keycap fidget app?

Keyz is a keycap fidget app — a pocket-sized set of mechanical keyboard keycaps you tap for the satisfying click, clack, and thock of real switches. There's no typing and no rules: just tap the keys, feel the haptics, and hear the sound.

Is Keyz free?

Yes. Keyz is free to download on the App Store and Google Play, and all sound packs are included for free. An optional in-app purchase removes ads and unlocks the secret keycap skins.

Does Keyz work offline?

Yes. Keyz runs entirely on your device — no account and no internet connection required.

What sounds and customization does Keyz have?

Keyz includes 7 mechanical keyboard sound packs and lets you customize keycap labels, images, and skins. You can also unlock secret skins by reaching tap milestones.

What devices does Keyz support?

Keyz works on iPhone running iOS 17 or later and Android devices running Android 12 or later.
