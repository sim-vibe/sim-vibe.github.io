Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Keyz is a keycap fidget app developed by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Keyz app.

2. Information We Collect

Keyz is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect personal information on our servers. The following data is handled locally on your device:

  • Click counts and app settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Custom keycap images you upload are stored only on your device.

Third-party services integrated in the app may collect data as described below:

  • Google AdMob may collect advertising-related data to serve ads.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit processes in-app purchases on your behalf.

3. How We Use Information

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data on our servers. All app data remains on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Keyz uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for serving advertisements. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Apple StoreKit — for processing in-app purchases. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

5. Children's Privacy

Keyz does not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13. The app includes advertisements, which may be shown to all users. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

6. Data Retention

All app data (click counts, settings, custom images) is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not store personal data on our servers, we are unable to process requests to access, modify, or delete personal information. All data is under your control on your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at keyz.dev/privacy.html.
